Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Creative Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website creativstudio.co and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit a quote request, or interact with our advertising.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and city when you submit our quote request form
  • Project details, timeline preferences, and uploaded photos or files
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide via email, phone, or in-person communication

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • UTM parameters and campaign attribution data from advertising links

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote requests and provide project estimates
  • To communicate with you about your projects or inquiries
  • To send follow-up emails related to your submission
  • To improve our website, services, and advertising effectiveness
  • To measure and optimize our marketing campaigns
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Tracking Technologies

We use the following third-party tools to analyze website traffic and measure advertising performance:

Technology Provider Purpose
Google Tag Manager Google Tag management and event tracking
Meta Pixel Meta (Facebook) Ad conversion tracking and audience building
Google Analytics Google Website traffic analysis (when enabled)

These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect browsing data. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We share your information with the following service providers, solely to operate our business:

Service Provider Data shared
Website hosting Netlify Form submissions, server logs
Email notifications Resend Name, email (for auto-reply and lead alerts)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, typically for the duration of our business relationship plus any period required by law. Quote request data is retained to provide ongoing service and for our records.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Opt out of targeted advertising via Meta and Google ad settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@creativstudio.co.

7. Opt-Out Options

  • Meta ads: Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Google ads: Google Ad Settings
  • Browser cookies: Adjust your browser settings to block or clear cookies
  • Email: Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us directly

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption on our website and secure third-party service providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
